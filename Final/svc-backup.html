Backup &
Disaster Recovery.

The question isn't if you'll need your backup — it's when. Ransomware, hardware failure, accidental deletion, natural disaster — businesses that recover fast have one thing in common: a tested, verified backup strategy. Racom designs, deploys, and monitors your business continuity environment.

Get a Free Assessment (866) 220-1422

Automated Daily Backups

Servers, workstations, and cloud data backed up automatically — daily incremental, weekly full, offsite copies in geographically redundant cloud storage. No manual steps, no forgotten systems.

Backup Verification

Every backup job verified every night — not just "did it complete" but "can we actually restore from this." Automated restore testing catches corrupt backups before you need them.

Ransomware-Proof Storage

Immutable, air-gapped backup copies that ransomware cannot encrypt or delete. Your last line of defense when everything else fails — tested and ready to restore in hours, not days.

Defined RTO & RPO

Recovery Time Objective and Recovery Point Objective defined, documented, and tested. You know exactly how long recovery takes — no surprises when disaster strikes.

Business Continuity Planning

Full BCP documentation — contact trees, recovery procedures, vendor contacts, and role assignments. We run annual tabletop exercises so your team knows what to do before a crisis.

Compliance Documentation

HIPAA, SOC 2, and financial regulation compliance requires documented backup and recovery procedures. We provide audit-ready evidence packages on demand.

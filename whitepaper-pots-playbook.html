POTS Replacement Playbook: From Legacy Lines to the Cloud

The retirement of POTS (Plain Old Telephone Service) is not a distant future concern — it's happening now. This playbook gives you a structured process for identifying every at-risk line in your environment, selecting the right replacement technology, and executing a migration without disrupting business-critical systems.

Phase 1: The POTS Audit

  • ▸Inventory every analog line — request records from your carrier if needed
  • ▸Classify each line by use case: voice, fax, alarm, elevator, modem, POS terminal
  • ▸Identify life-safety lines that require special handling (elevator, fire alarm)
  • ▸Document the hardware attached to each line and vendor contacts
  • ▸Establish a timeline for your highest-risk lines first

Phase 2: Selecting the Right Replacement

The replacement technology depends entirely on the use case. For voice lines, UCaaS (RingCentral, Dialpad, Zoom Phone) or SIP trunking. For fax, cloud fax services like eFax or RingCentral Fax. For alarm monitoring, cellular alarm communicators from your alarm vendor. For elevator phones, dedicated cellular elevator dialers that meet ASME A17.1 requirements. For POTS-dependent equipment, analog telephone adapters (ATAs) provide a bridge while you evaluate longer-term alternatives.

Phase 3: Migration Execution

  • ▸Start with non-critical lines to build team experience
  • ▸For alarm systems, coordinate replacement with your alarm vendor — don't attempt independently
  • ▸For elevator phones, require your building manager to coordinate with the elevator contractor
  • ▸Test replacement systems in parallel with existing lines before cutover
  • ▸Document every cutover date for billing cancellation

Cost Savings Calculation

POTS lines typically cost $40–$80/line/month for basic service. UCaaS replacements average $25–$35/user/month and include video, messaging, mobile apps, and analytics. A business replacing 20 POTS lines typically recovers migration costs within 6 months through reduced monthly spend alone.

Extend Your IT Team Without Replacing It

The Co-Managed Difference

From Overwhelmed to Optimized

Your IT team is stretched thin. Co-managed services provide backup without taking over.

The Problem

Internal IT Teams Are Drowning

✗
24/7 coverage impossible with a 2-3 person team. After-hours emergencies fall on the same tired staff.
✗
Skill gaps in cybersecurity, cloud architecture, and compliance. Can't hire experts for every niche.
✗
Strategic projects stall because fires consume all bandwidth. Infrastructure modernization sits on the roadmap for years.
✗
Vendor sprawl and chaos — multiple support contracts, no single throat to choke when something breaks.
✗
Burnout and turnover risk. Your senior admin leaves and takes 10 years of tribal knowledge with them.
The Solution

Co-Managed IT Partnership

✓
24/7 NOC monitoring & alerting handled by our team. Your staff sleeps, we watch. Escalate only what needs hands-on fixes.
✓
On-demand expertise in security, compliance, Azure, AWS, networking. Tap specialists without hiring FTEs.
✓
Overflow helpdesk support during peaks (mornings, Mondays, post-holidays). We take tickets so your team can focus.
✓
Project augmentation — migrations, rollouts, DR testing. We staff the project, you keep the institutional knowledge.
✓
Vendor consolidation. One partner for connectivity, cloud, security, backup. We manage the other vendors for you.
How It Works

Your Team + Our Team = Coverage

You define the boundaries. We fill the gaps. Clear handoffs, no turf wars.

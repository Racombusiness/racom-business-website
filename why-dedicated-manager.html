What Is a Dedicated Account Manager?

Your account manager is your single point of contact for everything Racom. They:

  • Learn your business, goals, and technology challenges
  • Proactively recommend solutions and optimizations
  • Coordinate all services - telecom, IT, security, cloud
  • Own escalations and ensure timely resolution
  • Review your technology roadmap quarterly
  • Act as your internal IT advocate

Why This Matters

Most technology vendors assign you to whoever picks up the phone. At Racom, your account manager:

  • ✅ Knows your infrastructure inside and out
  • ✅ Understands your business priorities and budget
  • ✅ Spots opportunities to reduce costs or improve performance
  • ✅ Manages vendor relationships on your behalf
  • ✅ Provides continuity - no more explaining your setup to strangers

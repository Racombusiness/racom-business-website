Understand Your Potential Savings with Software-Defined WAN

What is SD-WAN?

Software-Defined WAN (SD-WAN) separates network management from the underlying hardware, providing better performance, lower costs, and simplified management compared to traditional MPLS networks.

Typical Cost Savings

30-40%
Circuit Cost Reduction
50%+
Bandwidth Cost Reduction

Beyond Cost Savings

  • Better application performance with intelligent routing
  • Simplified branch deployments (plug-and-play)
  • Automatic failover and redundancy
  • Centralized security and policy management
  • Easy cloud connectivity without backhauling

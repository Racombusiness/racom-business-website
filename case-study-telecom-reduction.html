Case Study: 40% Telecom Cost Reduction

How one enterprise reduced telecommunications expenses by $250K annually

The Challenge

A 500-person enterprise was managing telecom services from 3 different vendors with inconsistent pricing, redundant services, and hidden fees. Annual telecom spend had grown to $625K without clear visibility.

The Solution

Racom Business conducted a comprehensive telecom audit and consolidated services to a single provider, eliminating redundancies and negotiating better rates based on volume.

Key Results

  • $250,000 in annual savings (40% reduction)
  • Single invoice instead of 3
  • Unified management through one account manager
  • Improved service quality with upgraded network
  • Better support with dedicated telecom engineer

Technical Details

The consolidation included: voice lines, SIP trunks, internet circuits, and long-distance services. We also upgraded their failover connectivity to improve reliability.

Client Quote

"Racom not only saved us a quarter million dollars, but simplified our entire telecom infrastructure. It's now one less thing to manage." - IT Director

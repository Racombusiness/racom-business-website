Attacks Stopped By Zero Trust + MFA

Real threats that can't succeed when you verify every access request.

Credential Stuffing

Attackers buy 10 billion leaked passwords from the dark web and try them across your apps. MFA blocks them even with valid usernames and passwords.

Employee clicks a fake Microsoft login page, enters password. Zero Trust requires device registration and biometric verification — phishing fails.

Attacker steals session cookies from public WiFi or malware. Zero Trust continuously validates device posture and location — hijacked session is rejected.

Insider Threats

Regulatory Requirements

MFA Is Mandated By Compliance

Zero Trust + MFA aren't optional — they're required for most industry regulations.

HIPAA

Healthcare

HIPAA Security Rule § 164.312(a)(2)(i) requires MFA for accessing ePHI. Covered entities and business associates must verify user identity.

PCI

Payment Card

PCI-DSS 4.0 Requirement 8.4.2 mandates MFA for all access to cardholder data environment. Applies to merchants and service providers.

SOC 2

SaaS / Cloud

SOC 2 Type II controls require MFA for administrative access and sensitive data. Auditors verify implementation during attestation.

CMMC

Defense

CMMC Level 2+ requires MFA (AC.2.016) for all users accessing CUI. DoD contractors must comply to bid on contracts.

Deployment Roadmap

Zero Trust Implementation in 4 Phases

We roll out Zero Trust incrementally to minimize disruption while building security layers.

1

Identity & Access Foundation (Week 1-2)

Inventory all users, apps, and devices. Deploy SSO (Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace). Enforce MFA for admins first, then roll out to all users. Establish baseline policies.

2

Device Trust & Posture (Week 3-4)

Enroll devices in MDM (Intune, Jamf, or Kandji). Validate device health before granting access — check for encryption, patches, antivirus. Block unmanaged BYOD devices or enforce compliance.

3

Network & Application Segmentation (Week 5-8)

Replace VPN with Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA). Implement microsegmentation — HR can't access finance, finance can't access engineering. Apply least-privilege to every app and resource.

4

Continuous Monitoring & Response (Ongoing)

Deploy SIEM and UEBA to detect anomalies. Log every access decision. Alert on impossible travel, unusual data access, or privilege escalation. Automate response playbooks for high-risk events.

UCaaS & Cloud
Business VoIP.

Your office phone system shouldn't be a fixed cost tied to a closet full of hardware. UCaaS puts your phone system in the cloud — fully featured, mobile-ready, and managed by Racom. We work with 14+ UCaaS carriers to find the right fit for your business and budget.

Get a Free Assessment (866) 220-1422

Cloud Phone System

Replace your on-premise PBX with a fully managed cloud phone system. Auto-attendants, ring groups, voicemail-to-email, and call recording — all managed from a browser.

Mobile & Desktop Apps

Employees use their cell phone or laptop as their work phone. Full business features, same number — whether in the office, at home, or traveling anywhere in the world.

Multi-Carrier Selection

Racom holds agent agreements with RingCentral, Zoom Phone, Microsoft Teams Voice, Dialpad, 8x8, Vonage, and more. We match you to the platform that fits your workflow and budget.

Number Porting

Keep every existing phone number. We manage the porting process from your old carrier — no disruption to customers, no missed calls during the migration.

HD Voice & Video

Crystal-clear HD voice calls and integrated video conferencing. No separate Zoom license needed — your UCaaS platform handles voice, video, messaging, and file sharing in one app.

Zero-Downtime Migration

We migrate your phone system with zero downtime. Parallel running, staged cutover, and same-day support on go-live day so your team is productive from minute one.

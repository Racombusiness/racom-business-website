Circuit Options

Choose the Right Circuit Type

From affordable Ethernet to ultra-reliable SONET, we'll help you select the circuit that matches your bandwidth, budget, and SLA requirements.

Metro Ethernet (EPL/EVPL)

Cost-effective Layer 2 connectivity over carrier Ethernet networks. Ideal for connecting branch offices to headquarters or linking data centers within the same metro area. Scalable bandwidth from 10Mbps to 10Gbps.

10Mbps – 10Gbps symmetrical bandwidth
99.5% – 99.9% uptime SLA
Layer 2 point-to-point or multipoint
Best for: Branch-to-HQ, campus connectivity

T1 / T3 (DS1 / DS3)

Time-tested TDM circuits offering predictable performance and low latency. T1 delivers 1.544Mbps, T3 provides 45Mbps. Still widely used for legacy systems, POS terminals, ATMs, and guaranteed voice quality.

T1: 1.544Mbps / T3: 45Mbps dedicated
99.9% uptime SLA standard
Fixed latency, low jitter
Best for: Voice PRI, POS, legacy apps

SONET / OC-3 / OC-12

Carrier-grade optical circuits built on SONET/SDH infrastructure. OC-3 provides 155Mbps, OC-12 delivers 622Mbps. Self-healing ring architecture ensures maximum uptime for critical applications.

OC-3: 155Mbps / OC-12: 622Mbps
99.99% uptime SLA (four-nines)
Self-healing ring protection
Best for: Financial, healthcare, government

Dark Fiber

Lease raw fiber strands and light them with your own equipment. Ultimate control over capacity, encryption, and network design. Scale from 1Gbps to 100Gbps+ using DWDM. Requires in-house optical expertise.

Unlimited bandwidth potential (100Gbps+)
You own/manage the optics and routing
Long-term cost savings at scale
Best for: Carriers, hyperscalers, large campus

Common Scenarios

When Dedicated Circuits Make Sense

Real-world use cases where guaranteed bandwidth and SLAs justify the premium.

🏥

Healthcare / HIPAA

Connecting clinics to data centers for EHR, PACS imaging, and telehealth. HIPAA requires encrypted point-to-point links — dedicated circuits avoid shared infrastructure.

Compliance Required
🏦

Financial Services

Low-latency trading platforms, core banking systems, and PCI-DSS cardholder data environments. Four-nines uptime and sub-5ms latency are table stakes.

High Uptime SLA
🏭

Manufacturing / SCADA

ICS/SCADA systems controlling assembly lines, robotics, and utilities. Real-time control loops can't tolerate packet loss or variable latency.

Real-Time Control
📞

VoIP / UCaaS

Enterprises with 50+ simultaneous voice calls need QoS guarantees. Dedicated circuits eliminate jitter and ensure crystal-clear call quality.

QoS Priority
🔒

Disaster Recovery

Replicating production data to offsite DR facilities. RPO/RTO targets demand guaranteed throughput for continuous data replication.

Business Continuity
☁️

Private Cloud / Colo

Direct connections to AWS Direct Connect, Azure ExpressRoute, or colocation facilities. Bypass the public internet for predictable cloud access.

How AI Ticket Triage Reduces Resolution Time by 40%

The Helpdesk Challenge

Support teams are drowning in tickets. Urgent security issues sit in queues behind low-priority password resets. AI-powered triage solves this by automatically categorizing, prioritizing, and routing tickets to the right resources.

How AI Triage Works

  • Analyzes ticket description and historical data
  • Automatically categorizes issues by type and priority
  • Routes to most appropriate technician
  • Suggests solutions based on similar historical cases
  • Learns from each resolution to improve

Real Impact on Resolution Time

Before AI Triage: Average 8-hour resolution time, 60% first-contact resolution

After AI Triage: Average 4.8-hour resolution time, 82% first-contact resolution

That's a 40% improvement in speed and 37% improvement in effectiveness.

Invoice Processing — Before & After

Real example from a 200-employee manufacturing company processing 500 invoices/month.

✗

Manual Process

1
AP clerk opens email, downloads PDF invoice attachment
2
Manually keys vendor name, invoice number, amount, date into ERP
3
Searches for matching PO, cross-checks line items by hand
4
If discrepancy, emails purchasing for approval via Outlook
5
Waits for reply (1-3 days typical), re-opens ticket
6
Manually routes to CFO for final approval if over $5K
7
Files PDF to network drive, marks invoice paid in ERP
8-12 min
Per Invoice
✓

Automated Process

1
Invoice email arrives, bot extracts PDF via API
2
OCR reads vendor, amount, invoice #, line items (2 seconds)
3
AI matches to PO, compares totals, validates tax rates
4
If match + under $5K → auto-approve, post to ERP
5
If discrepancy or over $5K → Slack alert to purchasing
6
Approved via Slack emoji reaction, bot completes workflow
7
PDF filed to S3 with metadata, audit trail logged
30 sec
Per Invoice (95% Touchless)

Case Study: 30% Lower Phone Costs with UCaaS

Migrating from legacy PBX to cloud-based unified communications

The Situation

A 200-person professional services firm was running an aging on-premise PBX system with expensive maintenance contracts and limited features for remote workers.

Migration to UCaaS

Racom migrated the organization to a modern Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) platform, eliminating hardware maintenance and providing better mobility.

Cost Savings

  • 30% reduction in monthly phone costs
  • $80K/year savings from eliminated maintenance
  • Zero hardware capital expenditure
  • Predictable monthly costs

Functional Improvements

  • Seamless mobile calling for remote workers
  • Integrated video conferencing
  • Better voicemail-to-email transcription
  • Advanced call analytics
  • Automatic failover for reliability

Implementation

Migration was completed in 2 weeks with zero downtime. All employees received training on new features.

Client Feedback

"Beyond the cost savings, our team loves having professional communication tools that work seamlessly with remote work. Racom made the transition painless." - Managing Partner

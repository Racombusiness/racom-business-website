The Complete Guide to MDR: What It Is, What It Costs, and Who Needs It

Expert insights on Managed Detection & Response services

What is MDR?

Managed Detection & Response (MDR) is a comprehensive security service that combines advanced threat detection technology with expert human analysis. It goes beyond traditional endpoint protection to provide continuous monitoring, threat hunting, and incident response.

Key Benefits

  • 24/7 Monitoring: Round-the-clock surveillance of your security infrastructure
  • Expert Analysis: Experienced security analysts investigating suspicious activities
  • Rapid Response: Quick incident response to contain threats before damage occurs
  • Cost Efficiency: More affordable than building an in-house SOC
  • Compliance Support: Helps meet regulatory requirements

MDR vs. Traditional AV

Traditional antivirus focuses on known signatures and basic protection. MDR provides behavioral analysis, threat hunting, and expert response to sophisticated threats that traditional AV might miss.

Pricing Models

MDR services typically charge per endpoint per month, with costs ranging from $3-15/endpoint depending on the provider and service level. Racom works with industry leaders to provide cost-effective MDR solutions tailored to your organization.

Is MDR Right for You?

If your organization has valuable data, regulatory requirements, or faces sophisticated threat actors, MDR is worth the investment. Contact Racom to discuss your specific security needs.

24/7 Remote
IT Monitoring.

Most IT problems don't start as emergencies — they start as early warning signs that nobody catches. Racom's 24/7 RMM platform watches your servers, workstations, network devices, and backups continuously — detecting issues and often resolving them before your team notices anything wrong.

Get a Free Assessment (866) 220-1422

Continuous Infrastructure Monitoring

Every server, workstation, switch, firewall, and cloud resource monitored around the clock. CPU, memory, disk, network — we see your entire environment in real time.

Automated Remediation

Common issues — disk cleanup, service restarts, Windows update failures, certificate expirations — are resolved automatically without a ticket. Your team never knows it happened.

Patch Management

Automated patch deployment for Windows, macOS, and third-party applications. Patches tested in staging, deployed in maintenance windows, and reported with full compliance documentation.

Backup Monitoring

Every backup job verified every night. We catch failed backups, corrupt restore points, and storage capacity issues before they become a recovery crisis.

Capacity Planning

Trend analysis on disk usage, memory consumption, and bandwidth — with reports that show you 30/60/90 day projections so you can plan upgrades before you hit a wall.

Monthly Reporting

Executive-ready monthly reports showing device health, patch compliance, backup status, and incident trends. Real data that shows what your managed IT investment is actually doing.
